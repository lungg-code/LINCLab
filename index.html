Our premise

01 / About

We study language as a window into the architecture of intelligence.

Our work brings together computational modeling, cognitive science, neuroscience, and artificial intelligence to ask how people understand, produce, and learn language.

By moving between human behavior, brain mechanisms, and machine models, we aim to build explanations that travel in both directions.
