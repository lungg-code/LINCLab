Language, Intelligence, Neuroscience & Cognition

LINC Lab Closes the loop between human language processing and machine intelligence.

01 / About

We are committed to fostering a welcoming, collaborative, and intellectually stimulating environment while advancing the frontiers of science and engineering.

Our mission is twofold:

  1. 01

    To understand human language comprehension in the brain through computable, testable models.

  2. 02

    To develop reliable and useful language intelligence systems guided by brain-inspired principles and interpretability, with applications in education, healthcare, and accessibility.
